VARIABILITY

An abrupt (<30 seconds) FHR decrease that does not meet the 15bpm depth AND 15s duration rule is considered variability and NOT a deceleration.

MAY NOT BE A DECELERATION: EVALUATE WHOLE CLINICAL PICTURE

For a deceleration to be prolonged, it must have a nadir ≥ 15bpm below baseline.
