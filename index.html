Canadian Fetal Health Surveillance Tools

Educational reference and logic-based tools adapted from the SOGC 2020 Fetal Health Surveillance guidelines.

About this Project:

This site was developed by a Canadian nurse to provide free, accessible educational tools for Fetal Health Surveillance. This is a non-commercial project: no data is collected, no user tracking is employed, and no monetary gain is derived from its use.
